KERV - KOMPA DRUM ENGINE  v1.0
by kimyondabeat
==============================

INSTALL (Windows, VST3)
1. Copy the "KERV.vst3" folder to:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
2. In your DAW, rescan your plugins, then load KERV as an instrument.
KERV works in any DAW that supports VST3 instruments.
To try it without a DAW, open Standalone\KERV.exe
(If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".)

HOME
- KERV opens on HOME: your kit drawn like a real drum set.
- Click an instrument to play it. Right-click CYMBAL = open cymbal, right-click CONGA = conga 2.
- Click [+] (or double-click an instrument) to choose its sound in the library.

EDITOR
- 12 drum categories: Conga, Cowbell, Crash, Cymbal, FX, Hihat, Kick, Percs, Rim, Clap, Snare, Tom.
- Every pad belongs to one category: a kick pad only takes kick sounds.
- Library: click a sound to hear it, star your favorites, search by name,
  drag a sound onto its pad or double-click it.
- PADS: 16 pads with Volume and Pan. Pads 1 to 16 = MIDI notes 36 to 51.
- SOUND CONTROLS: Pitch, Attack, Decay, Release.
  SOUND EFFECTS: Reverb, Delay, Filter.  RESET puts them back to default.
- SEQUENCER: 16 steps with a Kompa groove. It follows your DAW's tempo and play button.
  Click a step, right-click (or Shift) for an accent. KOMPA GROOVE loads the default pattern.
- QUICK MIX: volume, pan, mute and solo for every pad, plus Channel FX
  for the selected pad: 3-band EQ, Compressor, Saturation.
- LAYER: stack a second sound on a pad. Blend, Layer Pitch and Offset make it creative.

KITS
- 5 KERV kits: Classic Kompa, Modern Kompa, Afro Kompa, Zouk Love, kimyondabeat.
- NEW starts an empty kit, SAVE keeps yours (Documents\KERV\Kits). RANDOMIZE picks new sounds.

SETTINGS
- Interface size (75% to 130%) and Interface colour:
  KERV Purple, Black & Green, Grey & Orange, Cream & Cherry.
